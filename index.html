Sooner × Lean

Integration Overview

Sooner enables UAE homeownership through Forward Ijara — a Sharia-compliant lease-to-own model. Lean powers four critical touchpoints across the buyer journey: income verification, account validation, payment collection, and multi-party disbursements.

Buyer Journey

From Application to Homeownership

Steps highlighted in gold involve Lean integrations.

1

Inbound Lead

Buyer discovers Sooner via ads or website and submits a contact form

2

Pre-Qualification

Lean · Open Banking Data
  • Buyer completes qualification form (income, employment, property intent)
  • Connects bank account via Lean to verify income & generate 6-month statement analysis
3

Document Collection & Approval

Lean · Account Verification
  • Buyer uploads documents (bank statements, ID, payslips, salary certificate)
  • Lean confirms bank account validity before Sooner processes the application
  • Sooner issues approval decision: approve, conditional, or decline
4

Mortgage Pre-Approval

Buyer receives mortgage pre-approval via Sooner or a partner mortgage provider

5

Property Search

Buyer searches inventory, attends viewings, and selects a property

6

Sign Form B & Form F

  • Form B authorises Sooner as the buyer's agent
  • Form F is the MOU between buyer and seller
7

Full Mortgage Approval

Final credit committee sign-off and full underwriting complete

8

Sooner–Buyer Facility Contract

Buyer and Sooner sign the commitment contract for the lease-to-own structure

9

First Payment & Recurring Collections

Lean · Collections
  • Buyer pays first instalment
  • Lean collects recurring monthly Forward Ijara payments over ~60-month term
10

Disbursement to All Parties

Lean · Payouts
  • Sooner disburses funds to DLD, seller agent, and mortgage provider
  • Lean executes multi-party payouts with confirmation and reconciliation
11

Master & Sub-Lease Setup

Complete

Sooner signs master lease with landlord, sub-leases to buyer — Forward Ijara structure complete
