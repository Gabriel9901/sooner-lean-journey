Partner Integration Brief

Sooner × Lean

Integration Overview

Sooner enables UAE homeownership through Forward Ijara — a Sharia-compliant lease-to-own model. Lean powers four critical touchpoints across the borrower journey: income verification, account validation, payment collection, and multi-party disbursements.

The Full Lead Journey

11 Steps to Homeownership

From first ad click to master lease execution. Steps highlighted in gold involve Lean integrations.

1

Inbound

Lead Capture

Lead arrives via META ads or website. Fills basic contact form: name, phone, email.

Partner-submitted source
2

Pre-Qualification Form

Lean · Open Banking Data

Borrower completes full qualification form. Goal: determine if customer fits Sooner's criteria before investing more time.

Collects
Income, employment type, nationality/residency, property intent, existing liabilities
Lean Touchpoint
Borrower connects bank via Lean consent flow → income verification + 6-month statement analysis
3

Document Collection & Approval

Lean · Account Verification

Borrower uploads: bank statements, passport/Emirates ID, payslips, salary certificate.

Output
Internal credit score (SCS) → Approve · Conditional · Decline
Lean Touchpoint
Account Verification — confirm borrower's bank account is valid before proceeding
4

Mortgage Pre-Approval

Financing

Pre-license: refer to partner mortgage provider. Post-license: Sooner processes in-house.

5

Property Search

Real Estate

BDR/broker searches inventory, books viewings, client reviews units.

6

Sign Form B & Form F

Legal

Form B: authorises Sooner as buyer's agent. Form F: MOU between buyer and seller. Mortgage approval confirmed.

7

Full Mortgage Approval

Financing

Final credit committee sign-off. Full underwriting complete.

8

Sooner–Buyer Facility Contract

Legal

Sooner signs commitment contract with buyer. Legal prerequisite for the master lease structure.

9

First Payment + Recurring Collections

Lean · Collections

Buyer pays first instalment. Recurring monthly Forward Ijara payments begin over ~60-month term.

Lean Touchpoint
Collections — Lean collects recurring payments at 0.40% per transaction over the full ~60-month term
10

Sooner Disburses to All Parties

Lean · Payouts

Sooner pays: DLD fees, seller agent commission, mortgage provider.

Lean Touchpoint
Payouts — Lean executes disbursements to each party (DLD, seller agent, mortgage provider)
11

Master & Sub-Lease Setup

Complete

Sooner signs master lease with landlord, sub-leases to buyer. Legal Forward Ijara structure complete — same model as AirBnB ownership structure.
